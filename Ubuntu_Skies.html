Responsible Innovation Decision Worksheet

You can still complete the essential learning task. Use a notebook or a word-processing file. This pathway is formative and does not authorise real UAS operations.

Mamelodi Wetland Watch

Prioritise drainage and rehabilitation evidence while avoiding homes, footpaths and unnecessary personal data.

Makaleng Heritage Memory Map

Design a community-governed heritage record with public and restricted layers.

Vhembe Crop Resilience Cooperative

Use calibrated screening evidence to guide farmer-led field checks and irrigation decisions.

  1. People and purpose: Name the beneficiary, decision owner, stakeholder commitments and a proportionate problem statement.
  2. System design: Select an aircraft or a non-drone method, an evidence source, a decision-ready output, a team and a safeguard plan. State cost, time and trade-offs.
  3. Six gates: Record evidence for purpose/ethics, current airspace and operator requirements, site permission/safety, community participation, privacy/POPIA, and cybersecurity/data stewardship.
  4. Spatial plan: Explain how the route or field transect will cover the required evidence, avoid hazards and protected areas, return safely and keep a contingency reserve.
  5. Live decisions: Write how you would respond to changed weather or site conditions, people entering the area, low reserve, or evidence-quality warnings.
  6. Evidence protocol: Verify metadata and calibration, inspect defects, compare with ground and local knowledge, mask sensitive areas, classify and secure data, record custody and state limits.
  7. Public-value recommendation: Choose a small pilot, a redesign, or a non-drone method. Defend the decision and include one measurable impact indicator.
Final dossier headings: problem and public value; stakeholders and permissions; technology and output; governance pathway; spatial and operational plan; data stewardship; risks and alternatives; recommendation; impact indicator; next revision action.
